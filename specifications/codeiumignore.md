# codeiumignore

- **File**: `.codeiumignore`
- **Syntax**: gitignore
- **Source**: https://docs.codeium.com/faq#how-do-i-exclude-files-from-codeium
- **Reference**: gitignore

## Syntax

Uses gitignore syntax with no deviations to the pattern language.

## Deviations from gitignore

None (pattern syntax is identical).

## Notes

- Excludes files from Codeium’s AI indexing and autocomplete context.
- Codeium also respects `.gitignore` patterns in addition to `.codeiumignore`.
